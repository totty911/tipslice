Mental math for 10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, and 25%

Move the decimal one place for 10%. On $50 that is $5.00. Double it for 20%, which is $10.00. Add half of the 10% figure for 15%: $5.00 plus $2.50 is $7.50. Add that same half to the 20% figure for 25%: $10.00 plus $2.50 is $12.50.

Eighteen percent is 20% minus 2% of the bill. Two percent of $50 is $1.00, so 18% is $10.00 minus $1.00, which is $9.00. Eighteen percent of $80 is $14.40: 20% is $16.00, 2% is $1.60, and $16.00 minus $1.60 is $14.40. Fifteen percent of $80 is $8.00 plus $4.00, which is $12.00.

Sit-down figures from Emily Post, Pew, and Toast

For sit-down wait service, the Emily Post chart lists 15% to 20% of the pre-tax bill. Counter service, takeout, buffets, and bars are different rows on that chart. Those cases are the restaurant guide.

In Pew's August 2023 survey, for an average sit-down meal that was not exceptional, 57% said they would tip 15% or less, including 2% who would leave no tip. Another 12% said they would leave 18%, and a quarter said they would tip 20% or more. The service-by-service answers are on tipping by service.

Toast's Q1 2026 card and digital totals were 19.3% at full service, 15.8% at quick service, and 13.7% for takeout. Cash is not in that set, and Toast says those rates are gratuities on processing volume that includes taxes and fees. The rest of the figures are on tipping by service.

Tip percentage FAQ

What is 20% of a $50 bill?

Twenty percent of $50 is $10. The bill plus tip is $60, before tax. The shortcut is to move the decimal one place, which makes 10% equal $5, and then double it.

What is 15% of $80?

Fifteen percent of $80 is $12. Take 10%, which is $8, and add half of that, which is $4. The meal plus tip is $92 before tax. If you would rather leave 18% or 20%, enter 80 in the table above and read those rows side by side.

How do I compare 15% and 20% on the same check?

Enter the bill once. The table shows the tip and the bill plus tip at each preset rate, so you can see the gap without starting over. On $100 the difference between 15% and 20% is $5. On $40 it is $2. A custom row, such as 18%, appears when you type that percentage.
