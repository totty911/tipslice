About TipSlice

TipSlice is run by Matt Totty. It is a free, privacy-first tip calculator and a set of tipping guides.

Who runs it

TipSlice is run by Matt Totty. It is an independent project, not a restaurant group, a payroll company, or a delivery app. Questions and corrections go to hello@tipslice.com.

What TipSlice is

TipSlice is a set of free calculators for estimating a tip, splitting a restaurant bill, comparing tip percentages, working out sales tax, and converting a bill in another currency before you add a tip. You use them in a web browser. There is no account to create and no app to install. The tipping guides are the written half of the site: restaurants, delivery, salons, pre-tax versus post-tax math, splitting a check, travel, large parties, and how tip pools work for servers. Where a guide states a fact, it links the source, such as the IRS, the U.S. Department of Labor, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, or the Emily Post Institute.

The tools and the guides are for personal convenience. They are not financial, tax, wage, or legal advice. Tipping customs and sales-tax rates vary. Always check the receipt before you pay.

Where the math runs

The math runs in your browser. Bill amounts, tip rates, party size, and tax rates are calculated with JavaScript on your device. TipSlice does not upload those numbers to a server to store or process them, and it does not keep a user database.

If you use Copy split summary or Share, the link you send can include the amounts you entered so someone else can open the same split. That choice is optional. The privacy policy describes it.

How the site is supported

TipSlice is free to use. The site is supported by ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve those ads. The privacy policy explains the advertising cookies and how to opt out of personalized ads.
